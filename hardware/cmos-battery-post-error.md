# Resolving a POST Error Caused by a Worn CMOS Battery

**Date:** 2026-09-17


## Issue
A computer was experiencing a POST error and repeatedly turning on and off.

## What I Did
- Diagnosed the issue by observing POST error behavior
- Identified the CMOS battery as worn out, causing the boot failure
- Replaced the CMOS battery

## Outcome
The computer booted normally after the battery replacement.

- Hardware diagnostics
- POST error troubleshooting
- CMOS battery replacement
- Computer maintenance
