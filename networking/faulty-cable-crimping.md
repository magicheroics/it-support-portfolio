# Diagnosing and Repairing a Faulty Network Cable

**Date:** 2026-09-11


## Issue
A network cable was causing connectivity issues.

## What I Did
- Diagnosed the faulty cable
- Re-terminated the cable with a new RJ45 connector
- Crimped the connector and tested the connection

## Outcome
The cable worked correctly after re-termination, restoring connectivity.

## Skills Gained
- Cable fault diagnosis
- RJ45 termination and crimping
- Cable testing and verification
