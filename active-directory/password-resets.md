# AD Account Password Resets & Email Recovery (Recurring Task)


**Frequency:** Ongoing — performed regularly throughout the attachment

## Overview
One of my recurring responsibilities has been resetting passwords for Active Directory (AD) accounts belonging to both students and staff when they are unable to log in, as well as helping students recover access to their email accounts.

## What I Do
- Verify the user's identity before making any changes (following verification procedures)
- Reset the AD account password
- Assist students in recovering access to their email accounts
- Guide the user on setting up a new secure password
- Confirm the account/email is working after the reset or recovery

## Skills Gained
- Active Directory account management
- Password reset procedures
- Email account recovery
- User identity verification
- Customer/user support skills (students and staff)
- Handling a high volume of repetitive support requests efficiently
